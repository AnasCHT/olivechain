What a scan really does

Every record behind a batch is digitally signed by the organization that made it and chained to the record before it. When you scan, the whole chain is re-checked on the spot — and your scan is written to the ledger as a signed event of its own.

A ledger cannot taste oil: laboratories, scales and audits provide the physical truth. This app makes any later alteration of their records detectable.
