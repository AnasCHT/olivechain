The journey of this oil

Every step below was recorded by the organization that performed it and digitally signed. Records can be corrected, but never silently changed — corrections are shown.

What happened to the by-products

Pressing olives leaves pomace, leaves and stones. This batch's residues were weighed, tracked to a recovery partner, and turned into new resources — verified by mass balance, not by promise.

Who earned recognition

EcoPoints are non-transferable credits issued only for actions the system could verify.
